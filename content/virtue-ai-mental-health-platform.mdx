Virtue is a mental-wellness platform concept that explores how personalized digital tools can help people organize supportive routines and find relevant resources. The product is designed around discretion and user control, but it is not a diagnostic service and does not replace a qualified mental-health professional.

## Key Aspects

- **Tailored Mental Health Tools**: Provides users with tools and resources customized to their mental health needs.
- **Privacy-Conscious Design**: Treats user control, limited data exposure, and clear permissions as core product requirements.
- **Comprehensive Support**: Offers guidance for common mental health challenges such as stress and anxiety.

## FAQ

### What is Virtue?

Virtue is a mental-wellness platform concept focused on personalized self-guided tools, supportive resources, and privacy-conscious interaction design.

### Is Virtue a diagnostic or emergency service?

No. Virtue is not presented as a diagnostic tool, an emergency service, or a replacement for professional medical care. Anyone in immediate danger should contact local emergency services or an appropriate crisis resource.
