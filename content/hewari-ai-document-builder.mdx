Hewari—currently presented on its live site as **BA-AI**—is an AI-assisted workspace for common [business analysis](/blog/ai-in-business-analysis) deliverables. It helps users create structured first drafts of BRDs, PRDs, SWOT analyses, user stories, requirements, and stakeholder analyses. Generated material still needs a knowledgeable person to check evidence, organizational context, completeness, and approval requirements.

## Core Features

- **Structured document drafting**: Start BRDs, PRDs, SWOT analyses, user stories, and related artifacts from guided inputs.
- **Business analysis assistance**: Organize requirements, stakeholders, risks, and strategic observations into reviewable outputs.
- **Workflow-oriented generation**: Use multi-step prompting and tool-assisted reasoning where a task benefits from it.
- **Provider-flexible AI**: The product can evolve its underlying model configuration without making the workflow dependent on one model name.
- **Human review**: Treat every generated output as a draft that must be checked against source evidence and organizational standards.

## Why Choose Hewari?

- **Reduce blank-page work**: Begin from a structured draft rather than an empty document.
- **Make review clearer**: Organize assumptions and sections so analysts can identify gaps.
- **Keep judgment with the analyst**: Use AI for drafting and synthesis, not as a substitute for validation or sign-off.
- **Reuse repeatable structures**: Apply consistent document patterns across similar engagements.

## Use Cases

Hewari is ideal for a variety of professional scenarios, including:

- **Business Analysis**: Generate [detailed reports and insights](/bi) effortlessly.
- **Project Documentation**: Create workflows, timelines, and project plans seamlessly.
- **Operational Planning**: Build strategic and operational documents with AI support.
- **Review workflows**: Export or share drafts through the team's approved collaboration process.

### Who is Hewari for?

Hewari suits business analysts, project teams, and operational planners who need detailed reports, project documentation, or strategic documents with AI support.
