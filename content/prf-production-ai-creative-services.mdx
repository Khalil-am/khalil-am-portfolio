PRF Production is a creative-services concept that explores how AI-assisted tools can support traditional video production, photography, and multimedia workflows. It focuses on using automation selectively in editing and post-production while keeping creative direction and final review with people.

## Highlights

- **AI-Assisted Video Workflow**: Explore AI support for selected editing, color-grading, and effects tasks while a person approves the final output.
- **Photography Post-Production**: Explore assistive tools for image adjustments and retouching without treating automation as creative direction.
- **Personalized Client Experience**: Dedicated client portals and feedback loops to ensure creative needs are met efficiently.
