Wajibaty is an AI study tool that turns a topic or homework question into an interactive map of concepts. Instead of returning another long block of chat, it lays out the root idea, prerequisites, examples, common mistakes, and related applications as a graph that a student can explore.

The portfolio case study focuses on visual learning rather than general-purpose conversation. Availability and model behavior can change, so the workflow and its limitations are documented here as the durable reference.

## How the study graph works

A learner starts with a topic, a question, or a problem. Wajibaty generates a connected map that makes the structure of the subject visible. Selecting a node opens an explanation, key points, a worked example, and common mistakes; the learner can then expand that node into more detailed sub-concepts.

For homework questions, the graph can organize the prerequisite steps between the question and a possible solution. That makes it easier to see _why_ each step matters instead of copying a final answer without understanding it.

## Current capabilities

- **Interactive concept maps** organize prerequisites, examples, pitfalls, and applications around one topic.
- **Node-level explanations** let students expand only the part they need to understand next.
- **Question-led learning** maps the knowledge and steps needed to work through a homework problem.
- **Targeted quizzes** generate questions from the exact concepts explored in a study graph.
- **Model choice through OpenRouter** supports multiple available language-model providers.
- **English and Arabic input** allows the graph language to follow the learner's input.
- **A personal study library** lets learners return to earlier graphs and review them later.

## Why visual structure matters

Many subjects are difficult because their ideas depend on one another. A linear answer can hide those dependencies. A study graph makes the relationships explicit: what must be understood first, where an example fits, which mistake is common, and what concept should follow next.

Wajibaty focuses on that learning workflow. It does not replace a teacher, guarantee a correct answer, or remove the need to verify important academic work. Its purpose is to help students organize and interrogate a topic more actively.

## FAQ

### What is Wajibaty?

Wajibaty is an AI-powered visual study tool. It converts a topic or homework question into an interactive graph of concepts, prerequisites, examples, pitfalls, and applications.

### Can Wajibaty explain one concept in more detail?

Yes. A learner can select a graph node to see an explanation, key points, a worked example, and common mistakes, then expand the graph with related sub-concepts.

### Does Wajibaty support Arabic?

Wajibaty accepts English and Arabic input, and the generated study graph follows the language of the learner's prompt.

### Does Wajibaty complete homework automatically?

Wajibaty is designed to map the concepts and prerequisite steps behind a question so a student can understand the path to an answer. Important academic work should still be checked against course materials and teacher guidance.
