For years, enterprise business consulting has been weighed down by the same repetitive work: writing user manuals from scratch, assembling UAT test case workbooks, translating notification templates into bilingual sets, and replicating executive dashboards across environments. These are high-effort, low-creativity tasks that quietly consume days — sometimes weeks — of every engagement.

**BC Automations** was built to change that.

Live at [bc-automations.click](https://www.bc-automations.click), BC Automations is a growing library of **AI-powered automation templates** designed specifically for business consultants working with enterprise project management platforms — **PPlus, SPlus, and Diwan**.

> BC Automations is an independent portfolio and productivity project, not an official product of the named platforms or a former employer. Its public case study describes generalized workflow patterns and excludes confidential client data, credentials, and proprietary system details.

## What BC Automations Actually Delivers

Every template inside BC Automations turns a real consulting deliverable into a structured, AI-driven workflow designed to produce a review-ready draft. A consultant remains responsible for validating the source material, output, and final delivery.

### **User Manual Generation**

Generate structured user-manual drafts from authorized system material or business requirement documents across PPlus, SPlus, and Diwan. The automation handles much of the first-pass writing, screenshot organization, and formatting; a consultant reviews accuracy and completeness before release.

### **UAT Test Case Workbooks**

Turn a configuration manual into a structured UAT-workbook draft containing test cases, preconditions, expected results, and traceability. The responsible team validates the cases against the configured environment before execution.

### **Bilingual Notification Templates**

Use approved notification specifications or exports to draft bilingual Arabic and English templates. Language, placeholders, policy wording, and delivery behavior still require review before implementation.

### **Dashboard Replication**

Compare approved dashboard-configuration exports and propose a structured source-to-target mapping. An authorized operator reviews differences and applies changes through the platform's normal controls.

### **Group Permissions Automation**

Convert reviewed permission matrices into structured configuration proposals, reducing repetitive setup while preserving access-control review and least-privilege checks.

### **Migration Sheets for SPlus**

Turn approved source exports into migration-workbook drafts that capture structure, relationships, and data shape for review before migration planning.

## Why It Works: The MCP Layer Behind It

BC Automations uses a scoped **Model Context Protocol (MCP) layer** to expose approved product references as structured context. Instead of asking a model to guess how a platform works, the workflow retrieves relevant source material and keeps deterministic configuration and validation steps outside free-form generation.

This is what separates BC Automations from "generic AI prompts":

- **Grounded in approved source material** — drafts point back to the references supplied for the engagement.
- **Deterministic where it matters** — configuration, APIs, and mappings are treated as structured data, not free-text guessing.
- **Reusable and versioned** — consultants share templates, not ad-hoc prompts.
