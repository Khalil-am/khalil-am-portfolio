Keef Libsaty is a portfolio product concept exploring visual fashion discovery. A person starts with an image or product link, and the workflow uses visual characteristics to propose potentially similar catalogue items for review.

The concept is about reducing the gap between seeing a style and finding useful search terms or alternatives. It is not presented as proof of production availability, retailer coverage, recommendation accuracy, or a commercial partnership.

## Workflow explored

- **Image or link input** provides a visual reference rather than requiring a person to describe every detail in words.
- **Similarity suggestions** can organize candidate items using visible properties such as colour, shape, pattern, and garment type.
- **Catalogue links** can take a user to the retailer or source that supplied a matched item when that data is available.
- **Human choice** remains central because visual similarity does not establish quality, fit, availability, price, authenticity, or personal preference.

## Product considerations

A useful version of this workflow needs more than an image model. Catalogue freshness, regional availability, duplicate products, image rights, retailer attribution, and clear sponsored-result labeling all affect trust. The product should also let a person refine or reject suggestions instead of treating the first model output as authoritative.

Recommendation quality depends on the images and catalogue supplied to the system. Similar-looking items may differ materially in construction, sizing, price, or seller reliability, so results should be presented as discovery aids rather than guaranteed equivalents.

## FAQ

### What is Keef Libsaty?

Keef Libsaty is a portfolio concept for using an image or product link to explore visually similar fashion items. It sits alongside the rest of my [product work](/projects).

### How do the recommendations work?

The workflow compares visible characteristics in the supplied image with available catalogue information and proposes candidates. The quality of those suggestions depends on the data and still requires human review.

### Can I buy the items it recommends?

When a catalogue record includes a valid retailer URL, a result can link to its source. Keef Libsaty does not itself guarantee that an item is available, authentic, correctly priced, or suitable for purchase.
