Wathiq is an e-invoicing platform concept designed to help businesses move from manually prepared invoice files to a structured issuance, validation, archiving, and reporting workflow. The product brings invoice data, payment status, and configurable tax fields into one operational surface.

## What Wathiq delivers

- **Digitize Invoicing** — a clean, end-to-end flow from issuance to archiving, with invoices as structured data rather than flat PDFs.
- **Support compliance workflows** — configurable VAT fields, invoice validation, and export formats that can be aligned to a business's applicable requirements.
- **Improve financial visibility** — [reporting dashboards](/bi), paid-vs-unpaid status, and revenue views that make invoice activity easier to review.

## Scope and verification

Tax and e-invoicing rules vary by jurisdiction and change over time. Wathiq is not legal or tax advice, and its configuration should be reviewed against current regulator specifications before production use. The portfolio describes the product workflow; it does not claim certification by a tax authority.

## FAQ

### What is Wathiq?

Wathiq is an e-invoicing platform concept that organizes invoice issuance, validation, archiving, payment tracking, and reporting.

### How does Wathiq handle VAT compliance?

The product supports configurable VAT fields and invoice-validation workflows. Each organization remains responsible for validating its setup against the current rules in its jurisdiction.

### What financial insights does Wathiq provide?

Reporting views can show paid-versus-unpaid status, invoice activity, and revenue information based on the data available to the platform.
